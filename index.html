Missions

    Why this matters

    Every manufacturer solved the same problems. Freeze a whole look and bring it back in one press. Chain a string of moves onto one button. Build a look on one mix effects and feed it into another, which is re-entry. And send any picture anywhere with an aux bus.

    A panel only has so many physical rows. The frame has more mix effects than that, so each row can be delegated to whichever mix effects you need. The names change, the buttons move. The thinking does not.

    Work in pairs. Finish the missions on all three panels; progress stays on each one as you switch.
