Take It Apart

Every show you have ever watched is built from a few pieces: a background, the way it changes, and layers keyed on top. Here are three real broadcasts. Take each one apart, then build it yourself on the Acuity.

Created by Harold Garcia · Instructor, RTVF 065E Technical Directing · Lawrence Herbert School of Communication, Hofstra University

Four moves, every clipHow this works

01

Watch

Play the clip. Then play it again.

02

Call it

Before you open anything, call out every layer you saw, from the bottom up.

03

Take it apart

Open the layers one at a time and check yourself.

04

Build it

Go to the panel. Take the first three steps, then finish it on your own.

For every layer, ask the same three questions:

Decision 1What's included?
Decision 2What does it look like?
Decision 3What makes it happen?

"Makes it happen" is how you would build each layer on the Acuity. The broadcaster's own control room may do it with other gear.
